E1: shared boundaries, gaps and updates

Spaghetti storage keeps two copies of a shared boundary; topological storage keeps one arc. These panels draw the stored E1 files for two adjacent polygons on synthetic data.

Each panel shows the full 0.01-degree height, but its width is stretched around the shared boundary so that small differences are visible. Horizontal and vertical scales therefore differ, and panel 2 uses a much narrower width. Distances and areas are computed in UTM 48N (EPSG:32648).
